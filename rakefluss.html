Change the rake. See who pays it.

Load a real table, move the rake percentage or the cap, and everything below re-computes: what each player pays, what he keeps, and what is left for the room after rakeback and bonuses.

For example 25/50 has a €20 cap today, 0.40 bb. Type 0.60 into the cap field and you see straight away who pays the extra and how much more the room keeps.

Load real players from hand histories

A block is a piece of JSON computed from real hand histories. It fills in the seats, every player's money in per hand, their VPIP and their measured effective rake rate, plus the saw-flop percentage and the median pot — so you are testing a structure against a real table instead of an invented one. Your rake % and cap are never overwritten. Paste one block or a whole file with several; nothing is applied until you press Import.

Fill the regs in from the whale

Give the whale a winrate and press the button. The regs get random winrates that add up to exactly what he loses, so the table stays zero-sum. Press again for a new draw. Spread controls how far apart they land; where the seats have inv figures, the draw is centred on each reg's share of the money instead of on an equal share.

04Reg ceiling

The highest win rate you are willing to let a reg keep. Set it and the app tells you the cap that would get you there.
